Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Tamara Mohammed

Dentist & Clinic Owner

With over 20 years of dental experience, Dr. Mohammed has dedicated her career to bringing patients genuine, lasting results in a warm and caring environment. She graduated with honours with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Baghdad, and later earned her Canadian DDS from the University of Toronto.

What drives her most: seeing a patient who was once in pain walk out smiling with confidence. She takes the time to listen, explains clearly, and treats every patient the way she would treat her own family.
